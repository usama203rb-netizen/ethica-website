About ETHICA

Healthcare revenue experts with ethical, measurable workflows

ETHICA REVENUE GROUPS is a Lahore-based medical billing and revenue cycle company focused on helping providers improve reimbursement accuracy and financial stability.

Our Mission

To provide transparent and quality-focused medical billing services that reduce administrative pressure on healthcare providers while improving collection performance.

Our Vision

To become a trusted revenue cycle partner for clinics, specialists, and healthcare institutions through ethical operations, clear reporting, and consistent results.

Core Values

  • Integrity in every claim, report, and client interaction.
  • Accountability with clear reporting and KPI ownership.
  • Confidentiality and strict patient data protection.
  • Continuous improvement through regular team training.

What we deliver

End-to-end revenue cycle support, plus optional technology services when practices need digital tools alongside billing operations.

Revenue cycle services

  • Patient registration and insurance verification
  • Medical coding (CPT, ICD-10, HCPCS)
  • Claim submission, payment posting, and reconciliation
  • Denial management, appeals, and aged A/R follow-up
  • Patient billing support and KPI reporting

Technology services

  • Business websites, portals, and web applications
  • Android and iOS app development
  • AI automation, chatbots, and workflow tools
