Performance metrics we track and report

Like leading RCM partners, we manage to defined KPIs — not vague promises. Each figure below is tied to a measurable workflow and shared in weekly or monthly client reports.

98%
First-pass clean claim target

Measured as claims accepted without correction on first submission after payer-rule scrubbing, coding validation, and demographic checks before filing.

48 hrs
Average claim turnaround

Tracked from charge readiness to electronic submission for standard encounters, excluding holds waiting on provider documentation or authorization.

HIPAA
Security-first operations

Access is limited to assigned staff, transmission uses encrypted channels, and PHI handling follows documented confidentiality and minimum-necessary controls.

Denial root-cause work

Denied and rejected claims are queued by reason code, corrected, appealed when appropriate, and resubmitted with a documented recovery trail.

A/R aging discipline

Aging buckets drive follow-up priority so open balances do not stall between posting, payer inquiry, and appeal cycles.

Transparent reporting

Clients receive dashboards covering clean claim rate, days in A/R, first-pass acceptance, denial trends, and monthly collections performance.

KPI definitions and report cadence are confirmed during onboarding so every practice can verify results against the same measurement rules.
